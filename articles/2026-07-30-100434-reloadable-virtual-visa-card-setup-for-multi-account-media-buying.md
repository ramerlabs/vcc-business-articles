# reloadable virtual visa card:setup for multi-account media buying

<p>Managing several advertising accounts can make payment operations harder than campaign operations. Each account may have separate spending limits, billing dates, verification requirements, and internal owners. When one payment method is attached everywhere, a declined transaction, account review, or unexpected charge can disrupt more than one campaign at the same time. A reloadable virtual visa card can help media buyers create a more organized payment layer, provided the card issuer and advertising platform allow the arrangement.</p><p>The goal is not to bypass platform checks or hide account ownership. It is to separate legitimate business expenses, control exposure, and make balances easier to monitor. It can also reduce the operational impact of a payment issue. If one card reaches its approved budget or requires a billing review, other cards may remain available for unrelated campaigns.</p><p>That separation is useful, but it is not a substitute for platform compliance. Advertising networks may review the business identity, payment profile, domain, account history, and relationship between related accounts. A card should be issued to an appropriate legal or business user, funded from a legitimate source, and used according to the platform's terms. The strongest setup combines payment separation with accurate records and consistent account information.</p><h2>Plan the account and card architecture first</h2><p>Start by mapping the accounts you actually manage. Avoid creating unnecessary cards for every small campaign, because excessive complexity can make approvals, funding, and reconciliation more difficult.</p><p>A practical structure might assign one card to each client with a high or variable budget, while grouping smaller campaigns by one clearly documented business unit.
